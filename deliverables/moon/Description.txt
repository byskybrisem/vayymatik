What if the Moon suddenly started falling toward Earth? A hypothetical scenario in which the Moon's orbit is somehow altered.

As the Moon approached, extreme tides could flood coastal cities. Powerful tidal forces could tear the Moon apart before it ever reached the surface, creating a ring of debris — and some of that debris could rain down on Earth. A direct collision, if it occurred, could devastate the surface. Outcomes shown are possibilities, not predictions.
